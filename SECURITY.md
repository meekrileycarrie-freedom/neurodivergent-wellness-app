# Security and Privacy Overview

This app is designed to protect the privacy and well-being of its neurodivergent and spiritually sensitive users.

## Key Policies
- **No diagnosis or medical advice.** AI interactions are strictly for self-help, journaling, and gentle reflection.
- **Data encryption:** All sensitive user data (journals, AI chat, settings) are encrypted in-transit and at-rest.
- **Responsible logging:** Conversation and analytics logs are anonymized or opt-in only.
- **User control:** Users may export, anonymize, or permanently delete their data.
- **Authentication:** OAuth2 or equivalent secure login, option for pseudonymous access.
- **2FA:** Users strongly encouraged to enable two-factor authentication.
- **Crisis boundaries:** Built-in resources for mental health emergencies; escalation guidance, never crisis intervention by the app or AI.
- **Compliance:** GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA-awareness for all data handling.
- **Vulnerability reporting:** Please report security issues via GitHub Issues or the designated security contact in this file. Security reviews will be integrated into every PR.
