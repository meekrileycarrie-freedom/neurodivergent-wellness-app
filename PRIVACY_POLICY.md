# Privacy Policy

Inner Work: A Journey to Wholeness is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your information:

- **Data Collection:** We collect only essential information for account, journaling, and security purposes.
- **Data Protection:** All personal data is encrypted and stored with industry-standard security.
- **User Control:** You can request data export or deletion at any time.
- **Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
- **Cookies:** Minimal cookies are used for session security.
- **Compliance:** The app complies with GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA guidelines where applicable.

Questions? Contact support within the app.
